# 4. The quantum foundations

Quantum mechanics redefined the physical basis of computation. Planck introduced quantized energy, Einstein gave form to the concept of photons, Bohr redefined the atom in terms of state transitions, and Heisenberg, Schrödinger, Born, and von Neumann developed the mathematical architecture of the theory. This is the basis for the qubit: a state that can combine possibilities rather than simply choose between two states.
