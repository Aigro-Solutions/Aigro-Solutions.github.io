# 1. Introduction

This paper addresses a set of questions that are increasingly central to both technology and public policy: how do artificial intelligence and quantum computing affect human work, professional judgment, and human agency? The project begins from a simple but consequential premise: the key issue is not whether automation will replace people in a total sense, but how tasks, professions, and decision structures will be reorganized.

The project therefore adopts a human-centered framework. It distinguishes between fact, hypothesis, roadmap, and scenario, and it preserves the role of the person as a decision-maker, critic, and moral agent.
