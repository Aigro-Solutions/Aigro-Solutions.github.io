# 2. Methodology and editorial rigor

This paper adopts a strict distinction between several categories of statement:

- Fact: established historical or scientific evidence;
- Interpretation: explanatory reading of the evidence;
- Hypothesis: a proposal still under empirical validation;
- Roadmap: a business or engineering plan, not a scientific guarantee;
- Scenario: a plausible future trajectory, not a prediction;
- Editorial opinion: a position taken under explicit acknowledgment.

This distinction is crucial because the public discussion around AI and quantum computing often conflates commercial claims with scientific reality.
